Feature-code automation

Codes in the point list become blocks (symbols) and linework. Rule line: code,type(P/L/G),layer,symbol,color,AutoCAD colour no.,chainage(1). String number goes after the code (CL1, RE-L2); put END or CLOSE in the code/description to end or close a string. Points are joined in ascending numeric Point ID.

Horizontal alignment, setting-out and offsets

One PI per line: E,N,R (R = curve radius, leave blank or 0 for no curve; ignored at the first and last PI). Arcs are circular; no spirals. Offset: positive = right of the direction of chainage, negative = left.
